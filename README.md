# EmotionBuilder Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

EmotionBuilder is a mood journal that pairs how you're feeling with calming music. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Journal entries**: the emotion, intensity, and any note you write, along with the date and time.
- **Heart rate**: if you allow Health access, EmotionBuilder reads your most recent heart rate from Apple Health to record alongside an entry. The app only reads this data — it never writes to Health.
- **Saved songs**: songs you like are saved locally, along with the mood you were logging when you liked them.

All of the above is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). It is not uploaded to any server, except as described below for test builds.

## Apple Music

If you have an Apple Music subscription, EmotionBuilder checks your subscription status and streams a full song through Apple's own Apple Music service. This playback is handled entirely by Apple's MusicKit framework — EmotionBuilder does not see or store your Apple Music account details.

## Test builds only: shared with the developer

If you're using a TestFlight beta version of EmotionBuilder, journal entries and mood check-ins are also sent to a private spreadsheet accessible only to the developer, to help improve the app during testing. This includes the emotion, intensity, note, timestamp, and an optional nickname you can choose to provide. This sharing only happens in test builds you've explicitly agreed to when the app first launches, and is not part of the public release of the app.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
- We don't use third-party analytics or ad-tracking SDKs.
- We don't require an account or collect your name, email, or contact information (outside the optional nickname mentioned above for test builds).
